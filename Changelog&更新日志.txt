Neo Voxy 更新日志 / Changelog

## 0.4.3-beta.2（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）— 2026-09-05

### 简体中文

- 修复已加载 LOD 最远边界把未知邻区当作空气、为水体生成向上黑色立面的问题；真实水岸与已加载邻区的水体侧面保持正常。
- 修复不同维度及高 Y 坐标下各级 LOD 地形逐级抬升，并保持表层方块材质与高度一致。
- 统一逐级降采样时的天空光与方块光传递，修复相邻 LOD 环明暗交替。

### English

- Fixed upward dark water walls at the far edge of loaded LOD data by distinguishing unavailable neighbours from real air; real shorelines and loaded-neighbour fluid faces remain intact.
- Fixed progressive terrain uplift across LOD levels in other dimensions and at high Y coordinates while preserving the correct surface material and height.
- Stabilized sky-light and block-light propagation during successive downsampling, fixing alternating brightness between adjacent LOD rings.

- Some code was completed with AI assistance and will be audited and lightly edited by the maintainer before release to improve development efficiency.

## 0.4.3-beta.1（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）— 2026-08-23

### 简体中文

- Domum-Ornamentum 兼容由初步支持提升为完全兼容：特殊组合方块使用精细独立 LOD 模型，正确保留材质、方向、拼接几何、光照与持久缓存。
- 修复远景信标光柱靠近原版视距边界时短暂消失：只有在原版信标实体和有效光柱均已就绪后才交接，并保留安全重叠距离。
- 信标列缓存缺失时改为后台低优先级预热；重算期间保留上一份完整光柱，避免存储读取阻塞渲染线程或产生闪烁。
- 信标宽度改为交界处平缓、极远处持续增长且无硬上限的连续曲线，降低刚超出原版距离时的突兀增粗。
- 远景信标光柱接入 Iris 阴影图阶段；仅对已缓存光柱提交轻量棱柱阴影几何。

### English

- Promoted Domum-Ornamentum from preliminary to full compatibility. Composite blocks now use detailed dedicated LOD models with correct materials, orientation, joined geometry, lighting, and persistence.
- Fixed distant beacon beams disappearing while approaching the vanilla boundary. Handoff now waits for a loaded beacon block entity with a valid vanilla beam and keeps a conservative overlap.
- Cold beacon columns now warm asynchronously at low priority. The last complete beam remains visible during a rebuild, avoiding render-thread storage reads and transient blinking.
- Replaced the beacon width step with a smooth, unbounded curve that grows gently near the handoff and remains visible at extreme distance.
- Added distant beacon shadow casters to the Iris shadow-map pass using lightweight cached prism geometry.

## 0.4.2-beta.1（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）— 2026-08-22

### 简体中文

- 新增 LittleTiles 静态小方块远景兼容：区块摄取时一次性量化为 1/8 格稀疏表面网格，保留方块纹理、LittleTiles 颜色、群系染色与光照，并剔除内部相邻面。
- 小方块网格随 Voxy 世界缓存持久化，每 tick 最多上传两个变化区段；LittleTiles 未安装时不注册相关渲染器。
- 修复重进世界时尚未就绪的 LittleTiles 方块实体误删持久化网格，以及原版视距与 LOD 交界处的小方块不可见带；交接改为带安全重叠的圆形距离判定。
- 修复 LittleTiles 远景仅存在于内存、执行 `/voxy reload` 或原地重进后消失的问题；快照更新会可靠写入 Voxy 辅助存储，并在重载、退出与同世界存储切换前执行检查点。
- 修复 LittleTiles 远景被固定满光照导致夜间或阴影中过亮；现使用快照保存的天空光、方块光和原版动态光照贴图。小方块跳过透明 LOD 淡入，并在淡入起点进行三维硬交接。
- 版本更新至 0.4.2-beta.1；四个版本的模组元数据与配置界面名称统一为 `Neo-Voxy`，并同步更新中英文兼容性表格。

### English

- Added distant rendering for static LittleTiles microblocks. Geometry is captured once during section ingestion, quantized into sparse 1/8-block surface meshes, and keeps block textures, LittleTiles colours, biome tinting, lighting, and internal-face culling.
- Microblock meshes persist with the Voxy world cache and upload at most two changed sections per tick. Without LittleTiles, no related renderer is registered.
- Fixed persisted LittleTiles meshes being deleted when block entities were not ready during world entry, and removed the invisible handoff band with a conservative circular vanilla/LOD overlap.
- Fixed LittleTiles distant meshes existing only in memory and disappearing after `/voxy reload` or rejoining in place. Snapshot updates are now persisted defensively and checkpointed before reload, logout, and same-world storage replacement.
- Fixed LittleTiles distant meshes being forced to full brightness. They now use captured sky/block light with the live vanilla light map, skip translucent LOD fading, and perform a hard 3-D handoff at the fade start.
- Updated the integrations edition to 0.4.2-beta.1, standardized the mod metadata and configuration-screen name across all four editions as `Neo-Voxy`, and refreshed both compatibility tables.

## 0.3.1（1.20.1 Forge / 26.1.2 NeoForge 客户端版）— 2026-08-20

### 简体中文

- 将 1.21.1 联动版的水面基准校正同步至 1.20.1 与 26.1.2，避免粗粒度 LOD 像素把连续水面切成高低不一的接缝。
- 将 26.1.2 的扩展区块请求从渲染设置中拆分为独立配置页，与其他客户端版本保持一致。
- 修复 1.20.1 与 26.1.2 原版及模组树叶在 LOD 中染色掩码、叶片识别或模型去重失效导致的浅色、透明感和零散黑块；群系染色叶片也统一走稳定 tint 路径。
- 修复藤蔓及其他被错误声明为 SOLID 的透明植物/薄片方块在 LOD 中变成黑色矩形的问题，烘焙阶段会按实际图集像素自动启用 alpha discard。
- 补齐 26.1.2 的雾气配置选项，并修复 1.20.1 无光影时 LOD 距离雾距离过远而几乎不可见的问题。
- 将 26.1.2 的叶片识别与无光影染色修复补齐到 1.20.1；修复未注册原版颜色提供器的樱花树叶被错误染成黑色的问题。
- 彻底移除 1.20.1 的 LOD 边界淡入路径，因为其造成了严重问题，并修复 Sodium 地形矩阵捕获时机导致的远景拖影填充天空问题。

### English

- Ported the integrations edition's water-datum correction to 1.20.1 and 26.1.2 so coarse LOD pixels no longer split continuous water surfaces into visible height seams.
- Moved extended chunk requests out of the 26.1.2 rendering settings and into a separate configuration page, matching the other client editions.
- Fixed vanilla and modded 1.20.1 and 26.1.2 leaves appearing washed out, transparent, or speckled with black blocks in LODs when tint masks, leaf identification, or model deduplication failed; biome-tinted leaves now use the same stable tint path.
- Fixed vines and other transparent plant/thin-quad blocks incorrectly declared as SOLID from becoming black rectangular cards in LODs; baking now enables alpha discard from the actual atlas sprite pixels.
- Restored the full fog settings group on 26.1.2 and fixed 1.20.1 no-shader LOD fog using a distance so far away that it was effectively invisible.
- Completed the 26.1.2 leaf-identification and shader-free tint fixes on 1.20.1; unregistered vanilla colour providers no longer turn cherry leaves black in LODs.
- Removed the 1.20.1 LOD boundary fade path entirely and fixed sky-filling terrain trails caused by capturing Sodium terrain matrices at the wrong time.

## 0.4.1beta（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）/ 0.3.0（客户端版）— 2026-08-18

### 简体中文

- 将 GPU 世界曲率统一移植至 1.21.1、1.20.1 与 26.1.2；曲率从原版渲染边界外开始，避免边界台阶和按区段跳动。
- 客户端版统一版本号为 0.3.0，联动版更新为 0.4.1beta，并修复 26.1.2 包内版本元数据。
- 统一三档树叶 LOD 模式及配置入口；保留隐藏内部面剔除。
- 向旧版降级移植水体优先的 LOD 降采样、占用率就近取整和光照选择，减少流体破碎与 LOD 系统性抬升。
- 进服消息改为每次进入世界显示，默认开启，并可在 Sodium/Embeddium 的 Neo Voxy 设置中关闭；消息会直接提示关闭位置。
- 将联动版最新圆形 LOD 淡入同步至三个客户端版：使用三维相机距离与世界坐标稳定抖动，水体独立交接，树叶跳过透明淡入，减少空洞、噪点及树叶消失后重现。
- 将扩展区块请求同步至全部客户端版，默认关闭并限制为 48 区块；移动时暂停扩张，静止后渐进恢复，避免集中请求拖垮帧时间。
- 将 LOD 群系水色混合同步至全部客户端版；在模型构建时写入紧凑调色板。
- 修复 1.20.1 与 1.21.1 无光影远景雾距离，补全 1.20.1 水下雾遮罩，并使失明、黑暗正确遮挡 LOD；26.1.2 保留新版原生雾路径。
- 同步逐面独立 mip、精确逐像素染色掩码、水体边界分类及相关降采样修复，改善植被、水体和原版/LOD 交界质量。
- 远景信标的完整缓存渲染继续由联动版提供，避免在缺少数据索引的客户端版引入无效选项。

### English

- Ported GPU world curvature across 1.21.1, 1.20.1, and 26.1.2. Curvature starts outside the vanilla boundary to avoid a seam and section-relative snapping.
- Unified client-edition versions at 0.3.0, updated the integrations edition to 0.4.1beta, and fixed embedded 26.1.2 version metadata.
- Unified the three leaf LOD modes and their settings while retaining hidden internal-face culling.
- Down-ported water-aware LOD sampling, nearest occupancy rounding, and light selection to reduce broken fluids and systematic LOD uplift.
- Join messages now appear on every world entry, are enabled by default, and can be disabled in the Neo Voxy Sodium/Embeddium settings; the message states where to disable it.
- Synchronized the integrations edition's latest circular LOD handoff to all three client editions: 3D camera distance, world-stable dithering, independent water ownership, and direct leaf replacement reduce holes, noise, and leaf disappear/reappear transitions.
- Extended chunk requests are now available in every client edition, disabled by default and capped at 48 chunks. Expansion pauses while moving and resumes gradually while stationary to protect frame time.
- Added build-time LOD biome water-colour blending to every client edition using a compact palette.
- Fixed shader-free distant fog on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, restored the 1.20.1 underwater fog mask, and made Blindness/Darkness hide LODs correctly. Version 26.1.2 retains its newer native fog path.
- Synchronized per-face mip generation, exact per-pixel tint masks, fluid boundary classification, and related downsampling fixes for better vegetation, water, and vanilla/LOD boundary quality.
- Full cache-backed distant beacons remain in the integrations edition instead of exposing a non-functional client-only toggle.

## 0.4.0-beta-2 (1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版) — 2026-08-17

### 简体中文

- 实验性 LOD 轻量光照启用失败时在聊天框提示具体回退原因。
- 增加 NeoVoxy 内置的 Eclipse Shader 482 Lite 补丁，无需修改光影包；保留其 GBuffer 契约并简化远景透明反射与材质输入。
- Lite 着色器在驱动编译失败时同步更新状态、在聊天框提示并回退。
- 合并 LOD 群系水体颜色混合，提供 0–7 混合半径和可选草木混色；默认仅处理水体。
- 方块图集读取改用原生缓冲区，规避大型图集同步读取期间固定 Java 数组导致的 JVM 崩溃风险。
- 模型烘焙线程补充中断退出、初始化资源回收与请求锁异常恢复。
- 构建阶段检查中英文语言键和 JSON 完整性。

### English

- Lite LOD shading now reports its fallback reason in chat when it cannot be enabled.
- Added a built-in NeoVoxy Lite patch for Eclipse Shader 482, requiring no shader-pack changes while preserving its GBuffer contract and simplifying distant translucent reflections and material inputs.
- Lite driver-compilation failures now update the visible status, notify chat, and fall back.
- Added LOD biome water colour blending with a 0–7 radius and optional grass/foliage scope; water-only by default.
- Block-atlas readback now uses native memory to avoid pinning a large Java array across a synchronous GPU read.
- Hardened model-bakery shutdown, constructor cleanup, and request-lock recovery.
- Added build-time JSON and translation-key validation.

## 0.4.0-beta-1 (1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版) — 2026-08-17

### 简体中文

- 加入实验性 LOD 专用轻量光影管线；光影包可提供成对的 Lite 不透明/透明程序，在远景减少逐材质光照开销。
- Lite 默认关闭，在 Iris 构建或重载光影管线时检查。
- 加入三层保护：设置页显示实际启用/回退状态；Lite 程序必须声明一致的光影包、测试版本与契约版本；未满足距离过渡条件时整套回退标准程序。
- 提供经声明验证的 Complementary Unbound r5.8.1 + Euphoria Patches 1.9.3 实验补丁模板；水体保留完整材质和反射路径。

### English

- Added an experimental shader-pack-supplied Lite LOD pipeline that can replace the opaque and translucent LOD programs as an atomic pair.
- Lite shading is off by default and is resolved while Iris builds or reloads a pipeline.
- Added three safeguards: visible active/fallback status, matching pack/version/API declarations, and an enforced safe distance-transition contract with full-pair fallback.
- Added a declared test implementation for Complementary Unbound r5.8.1 + Euphoria Patches 1.9.3; water retains its full material and reflection path.

## 0.3.3-1.20.1-alpha.1 — 2026-08-11

### 简体中文

- 完成 Forge 1.20.1 原生客户端移植的发布前稳定性修复，不依赖 Connector 或 Fabric API。
- 修复无光影时雾气选项不生效；区分水下等必要环境雾与可选的远景雾，并按 LOD 距离计算天空雾。
- 修复樱花等透明树叶在无光影时显示为黑块。
- 补全配置界面的中英文文本及 SSAO 模式名称。
- 加入地面植物交叉 LOD 模型、性能/平衡/质量三档树叶模式，以及按帧预算工作的 LOD 构建压力控制。
- 保留此前完成的原版/LOD 边界、水体侧面、Oculus 管线重载和光影历史拖影修复。
- 1.20.1 暂不包含圆形 LOD 淡入及任何模组联动；Oculus 光影支持仍为实验性功能。

### English

- Completed the release-preparation stability pass for the native Forge 1.20.1 client port, with no Connector or Fabric API dependency.
- Fixed shader-free fog settings; required medium fog such as underwater fog is separated from optional distant fog, and sky fog now uses the LOD distance.
- Fixed transparent leaves such as cherry leaves rendering as opaque black without shaders.
- Completed Chinese and English configuration text, including localized SSAO mode names.
- Added crossed ground-plant LOD models, Fast/Balanced/Quality leaf modes, and frame-budgeted LOD build-pressure control.
- Retained the existing vanilla/LOD boundary, water-side, Oculus pipeline reload, and shader-history ghosting fixes.
- The 1.20.1 edition currently excludes circular LOD handoff and all mod integrations; Oculus shader support remains experimental.
